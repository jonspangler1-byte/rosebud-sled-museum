ROSEBUD SLED MUSEUM: SAMPLE SITE IMAGE SOURCES
================================================
Sled photos on the site show sleds from Jonathan Spangler's own Rosebud Sled Museum collection.
They come from two of his files:
  - Vintage Runner Magazine, Tuxedo Racer Edition (/workspace/vintage_runner/full_issue.pdf, 61 pages)
  - "Runners & Relics - Sled Photos" (/workspace/vintage_runner/magazine_photos.pdf, 32 pages,
    one photo per page, no captions). Photo pages 1-27 are byte-identical to the images
    already extracted from magazine pages 51-61 (listed below); pages 28-32 are new.
Four period advertisements / catalog pages (public domain) are also used; see the last section.
No stock images and no images of people are used.

Collection photos come from "Runners & Relics", PDF pages 51-61, which the contents
page describes as "Ten sleds from a private collection, photographed and catalogued".
The owner confirmed these are his own sleds.
The magazine names no photographer and gives no credit line for those pages.
Captions on the site are copied from the magazine's own catalog captions.
